# Calculation and model methodology

Economic ownership is the product of percentages along each simple upstream path.
Disjoint legal shares can converge at the same natural person and are summed once
per unique relationship sequence. Multiple active records for a pair are treated
as conflicts, not additional shares. Cycles, disputed records, unknown percentages
and overallocated registers withhold combined values. No arbitrary 100% clamp
conceals inconsistent records. Legal-entity terminals remain unresolved. Trust
beneficiaries and voting/control relationships do not become economic ownership.

Known path estimates are evidence-linked and temporal. The default 25% threshold
is an illustrative candidate rule, configurable per jurisdiction. No legal UBO
finding is claimed. Cycles are searched up to 12 edges with at most 100 displayed;
centralities use the connected economic ownership component. Complexity is not
wrongdoing. Shared directors/addresses can be routine legitimate arrangements.

Primary scoring is a configured weighted sum of bounded deterministic features.
Unavailable reference/screening/history indicators contribute zero; coverage is
reported separately and weights are not renormalized. Thus the score is a partial
observed-indicator review priority; it cannot establish low risk or predict crime.
Record confidence and coverage are heuristic evidence-quality summaries, not
statistically calibrated confidence intervals.

The optional Isolation Forest fits 480 independent synthetic rows and reserves
120 for descriptive held-out novelty statistics. Labels do not exist; no accuracy,
precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, confusion matrix or probability calibration
is meaningful. These must be measured on legitimate, separately governed labeled
data with entity/group/time-separated splits before operational use. Future model
comparisons should use nested cross-validation, leakage controls and calibration.
SHAP is not claimed; visible model features are descriptive input values.

The seven LangGraph nodes use deterministic services and validated JSON envelopes.
The assistant uses evidence templates. No external LLM opinion influences scores.
Retry policies bound stage retries; model and graph connection absence are explicit.
Independent human review is enforced separately from automation.
